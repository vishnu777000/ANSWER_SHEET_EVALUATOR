Methods used

Concept and keyword matchingExtracts answer concepts, matches them to the reference, and scores the fraction of reference concepts covered.
Normalization and negationNormalizes common plural/verb forms and selected synonyms; detects some negated concepts so contradictions are not counted as covered.
Optional semantic modelThe app can use a locally cached Sentence Transformer alongside concept scoring. It is disabled for this benchmark to keep the reported results reproducible.

This project does not currently use TF-IDF. Benchmark cases are shown for transparency and do not measure mark-level agreement with human graders.
